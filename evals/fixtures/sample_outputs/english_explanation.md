# Explanation for Parents: Addition and Subtraction of Rational Numbers

## What is this topic about?

Addition and subtraction of rational numbers is a core topic in Chapter 1 of the 7th grade math textbook [Textbook p.15-25]. In simple terms, it covers:

- Adding and subtracting positive numbers, negative numbers, and zero
- The key rule: **subtracting a number is the same as adding its opposite** [Textbook p.18]

## Why do students find it difficult?

1. **Sign confusion**: (-3) + 5 and 3 + (-5) have different results, and the sign direction is easy to get wrong
2. **Unfamiliar with converting subtraction to addition**: 7 - (-3) needs to become 7 + 3, but many students calculate it as 4
3. **Conflict with daily experience**: Students rarely use negative numbers in everyday life

## 10-Minute Home Coaching Script

### Step 1: Start with a thermometer (2 minutes)
"Today's lowest temperature is -3 degrees, and the highest is 5 degrees. What's the temperature difference?"

### Step 2: Number line game (5 minutes)
Draw a number line on paper, starting from 0:
- "Start at -3 and move 5 steps to the right" → reach 2 [Textbook p.16]
- "7 minus -3 steps" → equals moving 3 steps right → reach 10 [Textbook p.18]

### Step 3: Mental math - 3 problems (3 minutes)
1. (-2) + 5 = ?
2. 3 - (-4) = ?
3. (-6) + (-3) = ?

**Key**: If your child makes a mistake, don't say "this is so easy, how can you not get it?" Instead say "let's walk through it on the number line again."
